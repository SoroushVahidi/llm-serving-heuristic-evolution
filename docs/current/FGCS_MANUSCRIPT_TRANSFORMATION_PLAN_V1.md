# FGCS Manuscript Transformation Plan V1

Status: executed in `paper/llm2026/main.tex`; audit date 2026-09-20.

## Story transformation

The withdrawn LLM 2026 selector/exploitability manuscript was converted into
a journal characterization paper. The main sequence is now:

`abundant-resource action collapse -> pressure-induced opportunity -> fresh
causal latency headroom -> objective sensitivity -> bounded real-vLLM support`.

Historical joint-240 portfolio and selector work is no longer the central
claim. It is retained only where it motivates separating opportunity,
causality, and predictability.

## Final RQs

1. How often do SBS and P6 produce different canonical executable actions?
2. How does KV, active-sequence, or arrival pressure change that prevalence?
3. When disagreement exists, how often does a one-step alternative reduce
   mean latency, and by how much?
4. How sensitive is the conclusion to ANWG versus latency, and what transfers
   to a bounded real-vLLM harness?
